# reflection.md

## Overall Learnings
    
Throughout this project, I have learned that I am capable of creating an app that feels personal, organized and overall, useful. At the beginning, I was not fully confident in knowing where to begin, but throughout the process, I got more comfortable with SwiftUI and how different parts of an app connect together. This app has a lot of pieces that go together, so it felt like such an accomplishment to make something so personal and useful. My overall takeaway from this project is knowing that I can use the skills learned in class to make my life and and more productive through my creations. 
    
## Challenges

A continuous challenge I face throughout this class is  communicating clearly with AI to get the outcome I want. Sometimes, I expect one prompt to r create something perfectly, but I learned that there is usually an extra step of debugging, explaining what changed or even asking for a different version at the beginning. Even though this can be frustrating, it also helped me understand my project and swift UI better because I had to pay attention to what was working and what still needed to be fixed throughout the process.

## Next Steps

I also would like to make collaborative albums. With graduating college, this can be such a fun way to keep up with my friends.
